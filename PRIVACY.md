## 3. What we collect, why, and our legal reason

**Your postcode.** You type it in, or, if you choose, we work it out from your location. We use it to show events near you. It is saved on your device only. Legal reason: it is needed to provide the service you asked for.

**Your search distance, filters, saved events and display settings** (distance units, text size, reduced motion). We use them to remember your choices. They are saved on your device only. Legal reason: needed to provide the service you asked for.

**A record that you accepted our Terms and Privacy Policy.** This is the version numbers and the date and time you accepted. It is saved on your device only, so we know when to ask you again. Legal reason: our legitimate interest in being able to show that we asked.

**Your location, only if you switch it on in Settings.** Your phone or browser asks permission first. We use your position once, rounded to about 100 metres, to find your nearest postcode, and then we discard it. We save the postcode, not the position. We never store a location history. Legal reason: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by switching the setting off.

**Analytics and crash reports, only if you switch them on in Settings.** If you do, anonymous information about how the app is used and when it crashes may be collected to help us fix problems. Provider: [Analytics / crash reporting provider, or write "none yet"]. Nothing is started unless you switch this on. Legal reason: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by switching the setting off.

**Technical data on our servers.** When the app loads, our hosting provider ([Hosting provider and location]) receives your IP address and basic details of your request. We use this only to run and secure the service. These server logs are deleted automatically after 30 days. We do not put personal data in our own logs. Legal reason: our legitimate interest in keeping the service running and secure.

**If you email us.** We keep your email and our reply so we can deal with your question. We keep it for [how long you keep support emails, for example 12 months]. Legal reason: our legitimate interest in answering you, or our legal duties if you are exercising a data right.

## 4. What we don't collect

- No accounts, passwords, names, phone numbers or email addresses (unless you email us).
- No location history and no precise location is kept.
- No advertising identifiers and no cross-app tracking.
- No data from anyone who tells us they are under 13.

## 5. Who we share data with

- **postcodes.io**, our postcode lookup service. It receives the postcode you enter, and the approximate position of that postcode area so we can find nearby places. If you use your location, it also receives your position rounded to about 100 metres, once, to return a postcode. This is how we turn a postcode into a place on the map. It sees your IP address, as any website does. See its own privacy information at postcodes.io.
- **OpenStreetMap map servers**, which supply the map pictures (called tiles). When the map is on screen, your device asks them for the tiles that cover the area you are looking at. They therefore see your IP address, the area of the map you view, and the address of our site. They do not receive your postcode. Their own privacy policy applies. The map is only loaded after you accept our terms, and only while the map view is open.
- **[Hosting provider and location]**, which hosts the app and our public pages.
- **[Analytics / crash reporting provider]**, only if you switch on analytics and crash reports.
- **Google Maps**, only if you tap Directions. The venue name is passed to Google Maps in a new tab and Google's own privacy policy applies from then on.
- **Event organisers' websites**, only if you tap a link to one. Their own privacy policies apply.

We do not sell your data. We only share it with a provider where that is needed to run the app, and we ask each one to protect it.

## 6. Cookies and similar technologies

The app saves your choices in your browser's local storage. This is strictly necessary for the app to work and to remember what you chose, so it does not need consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Anything used for analytics is only started if you switch analytics on.

The app loads its font and its map software from our own servers. Only the map pictures come from a third party, as described above. Your browser may keep map pictures in its normal cache so the map loads faster.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to see what we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to take a copy of it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent at any time. You also have the right not to be subject to decisions made only by a computer that significantly affect you. We don't make any such decisions.

- **See or take a copy:** Settings, then Export my data. This gives you everything stored on your device.
- **Delete:** Settings, then Delete my data. This removes everything stored on your device straight away. Because there are no accounts and no copy on our servers, there is nothing else to remove. If we ever add accounts, we will delete your account and its data within 30 days of your request and confirm to you when it is done.
- **Anything else:** email [Email address]. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator: [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/). We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
